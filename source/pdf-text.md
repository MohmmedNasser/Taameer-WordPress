ABOUT US
Taameer Plus Contracting L.L.C was established in Dubai in 2015 as a small technical services company and has grown steadily 
through out years of gaining exceptional experience in the market till today, Taameer Plus is a full-service contracting company
with well-trained staff and employees delivering high quality and standards, using the late of the art engineering processes.
Taameer Plus primary goal is to achieve the highest quality in all our operations without compromise. Our objective is to ensure 
smooth forward improvement of our company performance, while offering our customers a safe, cost-effective, and professional
service.
Our goals are the most sustainable in the world of business. Quality performance is the cornerstone and part of company
culture and is considered a personal responsibility of all employees. To maintain quality performance of our business units at the 
highest level, the following aims are pursued:
-To fulfill or exceed customer needs and expectations by delivering a quality project consistently and promptly.
-To maintain the commitment to continuous improvement of performance and communicate our goals and objectives to 
every employee.
-To promote a working environment where training and tools are provided so that work can proceed safely and efficiently manner.
-To implement a system of policies which are periodically reviewed to ensure the ability of all working groups to perform their 
work effectively.

OUR SERVICES:
 
Taameer Plus is a pioneer General Contractor in United Arab Emirates, construction solutions professional and cost-effective 
manners.
We are proud to deliver the best services in various fields of :
-Turnkey Projects:
Taameer Plus provides experienced and knowledgeable professionals to handle all phases of project construction. As a General 
Contractor, we handle all types and volumes of projects ranging from new construction to renovations. Our project management 
team maintains full coordination throughout all project phases with our field staff, and qualifed subcontractors delivering project 
on schedule with the highest standards of safety, and quality, to insure meeting the deadline of our projects.
-Design And Build:
Taameer Plus handles Design-Build projects, by entering into a direct contract with the client to provide all design aspects related to 
structural, architectural, and electromechanical works. Taameer Plus has the personnel, working together as a team, to evaluate 
alternative materials and methods efficiently, and accurately. Value engineering and construction ability are utilized continuously 
and more effectively, when designers and Taameer Plus work together as a team during the entire design process. . These steps 
ultimately save the owner significant amounts of money and time.
